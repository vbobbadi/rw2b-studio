RQ2 · Synthetic test, 3 seeds

3D-rendered occlusion beats 2D-pasted on occluded images

Errors on the 3D-occlusion test set, split by why a joint is hidden. Every fine-tune helps; 3D-rendered occluders (D) help most, including on joints the body hides from itself.

PA-MPJPE by joint visibility (mm)

Key gaps (robust in all seeds)

On 3DPW, which has little occlusion, D is 2.1 mm worse than C on PA-MPJPE but 1.7 mm better on MPJPE.

RQ4 · RQ5 · Synthetic test

One generalist beats four specialists everywhere

PA-MPJPE (mm) of every model on every test condition. Stronger color is better; the best cell in each column has an outline. GEN wins every column, including each expert's home condition.

Fair-comparison caveat: GEN has the same total parameters and compute as the four experts together, but each expert alone saw a quarter of GEN's training steps.

RQ3 · Synthetic test, 3 seeds

Picking "hard" training data did not help

Same 2,000-image budget, three ways to choose it. Random sampling wins on every test set. The bandit put 92% of its budget into heavily occluded images and still only tied random there.

RQ6 · Diagnose step

The problem detector is near-perfect in Blender and near-chance on real clips

AUC of 1.0 is perfect, 0.5 is a coin flip. Simple image checks (brightness, blur, box position) beat the trained detector on real video.
